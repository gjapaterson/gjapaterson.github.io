Fenwood Nest

A private home-energy dashboard.

Fenwood Nest is a personal, non-commercial program run by one household on its own computer. It reads that household's own devices — a Nest thermostat and a myenergi solar hot-water diverter — and displays them together on a single dashboard.

It is not a product, a service, or an app anybody else can sign up for. There are no accounts, no users other than the owner, and nothing is published.
